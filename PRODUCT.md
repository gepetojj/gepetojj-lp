# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary visitors**

- Founders and executives evaluating CTO-level technical leadership for partnerships, consulting, or hiring
- Recruiters and talent scouts assessing senior engineering leadership fit
- Potential clients for Link Soluções or product companies (Spark, SC Finance, Triglit, etc.)
- Technical peers reviewing open source work and engineering credibility

**Context**

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, referrals, GitHub, or search — often on mobile, with limited attention. They need to quickly understand who João Nobre (gepetojj) is, what he builds, and why it matters for business outcomes. The site serves PT-BR (primary), EN, and ES audiences with the same strategic positioning.

## Product Purpose

Personal brand site for **João Nobre (gepetojj)**, CTO at Link Soluções. The site exists to:

1. Establish credibility as a business-aligned technical leader (not just a developer portfolio)
2. Showcase products and companies where João applies technology, leadership, and product thinking
3. Surface open source experiments as proof of hands-on engineering
4. Drive high-quality contact conversations (`oi@gepetojj.com`, LinkedIn, GitHub)

**Success looks like:** a visitor understands João's value proposition within one scroll, trusts the work is real, and initiates contact — or shares the site with a decision-maker.

## Brand Personality

**Voice:** Outcome-driven, precise, operational. Speaks business and engineering fluently without jargon walls or false modesty.

**Three words:** Resultado · Liderança · Execução

**Emotional goals:** Trust, expert confidence, clarity. The visitor should feel "this person ships real outcomes" — not "this is another dev portfolio."

**Tone guardrails**

- Lead with business impact, then technical depth
- Confident without hype; specific claims over generic superlatives
- Personal (portrait, location, real projects) without casual influencer energy

## Anti-references

**Do not resemble:**

- Generic dark-mode developer portfolio templates (Inter/Space Grotesk clones, lime-accent SaaS aesthetic)
- Hero metric dashboards (2×2 stats grids as section scaffolding)
- Repeated uppercase section kickers on every block ("SOBRE" / "SKILLS" / "WORK" eyebrows)
- Identical card grids with icon + heading + blurb, repeated endlessly
- AI slop tells: glassmorphism defaults, gradient text, over-rounded cards (24px+), ghost-card shadows
- Corporate SaaS landing pages detached from a personal voice
- Editorial-magazine aesthetics (display serif + mono labels + ruled separators) unless explicitly requested

**Specific patterns to refuse:** numbered section markers as default scaffolding, side-stripe borders, text-only work cards with no visual proof of products.

## Design Principles

1. **Result over resume** — Every section connects technology to business outcomes; skills support the story, they don't replace it.
2. **Show the work** — Products and leadership need tangible proof (imagery, case framing, domain context), not interchangeable text cards.
3. **One voice, three languages** — PT/EN/ES share the same strategic positioning; translations must not drift in tone or claims.
4. **Distinctive craft** — Typography, layout, and motion should signal João Nobre, not a template category.
5. **Accessible by default** — A public portfolio must work with keyboard, screen readers, reduced motion, and mobile one-handed use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Target:** WCAG 2.1 AA
- **Keyboard:** Full navigation with visible `:focus-visible` indicators; custom cursor must not block keyboard users
- **Motion:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; reveal animations enhance visible defaults, never gate content
- **Touch:** Minimum 44×44px targets on mobile (locale switcher, nav, CTAs)
- **Contrast:** Body text ≥4.5:1 on dark backgrounds; no washed-out gray on near-black
- **Progressive enhancement:** Core content readable without JavaScript (reveal, cursor, scroll spy are enhancements)
